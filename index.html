RACE CRAFT

  • ERS — battery recharges under braking; deploy on straights for extra power.
  • DRS — rear wing opens in marked zones when within 1s of the car ahead (always open in Time Trial).
  • Tires — sliding overheats & wears the slicks; grip falls as they degrade.
  • Wet races — brake earlier, avoid puddles at top speed: hydroplaning is real.
  • Slipstream — follow closely to gain straight-line speed.
  • Jump the start and you'll be penalized. Stay between the white lines!
